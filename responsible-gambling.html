Responsible Gambling

Sports betting should be entertainment, not a way to solve money problems. Everything on this site — every unit tracked, every chart — is for informational and entertainment purposes only. Nothing here is financial advice, and no result on this site guarantees future performance.

National Problem Gambling Helpline
1-800-GAMBLER
Free, confidential support, available 24/7 · ncpgambling.org

Some Ground Rules

  • Only bet money you can afford to lose — never rent, bills, or savings.
  • You must be 21+ (or the legal betting age in your state/country) to wager with a licensed sportsbook.
  • Past results, including everything shown on this tracker, do not guarantee future outcomes. Every system runs cold.
  • Set a budget before you bet, and stop when you hit it — win or lose.
  • Betting shouldn't be used to chase losses, cope with stress, or fix a financial hole.
  • If gambling stops being fun, that's the signal to step back, not press harder.

Signs It Might Be More Than a Hobby

If any of these sound familiar, it's worth talking to someone or reaching out to a helpline — no judgment, just a resource:

Betting more than you planned to, more often than you meant to
Chasing losses with bigger bets to "get even"
Lying to people close to you about how much you bet or lost
Borrowing money, missing payments, or dipping into savings to bet
Feeling anxious, irritable, or unable to focus when you're not betting
Betting is affecting work, sleep, relationships, or your mood

Where to Get Help

Call or text 1-800-GAMBLER (1-800-426-2537) — free and confidential, 24/7.

National Council on Problem Gambling — resources, state-by-state helplines, and self-exclusion programs.

Gamblers Anonymous — peer support meetings, in-person and online.

Most sportsbooks also offer built-in deposit limits, cool-off periods, and self-exclusion tools in their account settings — worth setting up before you ever need them.
